# KatsuDon Editor v3.6.0

A new logo and app icon, a fix for the update loop, and the gameplay preview's fake sliders and kiai flash now follow osu! stable more closely.

## Updating to this version

From v3.4.2 or later, click **Update and Restart**. From v3.4.1 or earlier, download it by hand once; see the v3.4.2 notes.

## New

- **New logo and app icon.** A drum face in don red and kat blue, used for the window, the exe and the README banner.

## Fixed

- **The update loop.** v3.5.10 and v3.5.11 reported themselves as 3.5.9, so every install kept being offered the update it had just installed. The release build now takes its version from the tag.
- **Fake sliders in the gameplay preview.** The head vanishes when the cap reaches the hit position; the body and cap then scroll on for 200 of slider length, stop, and fade over 500 ms, as in stable. A slider written 0.1 long or less (for example `+0.0001`) disappears at the hit position instead of passing through. A fast short fake slider whose cap is under 1 ms ahead (Lunatic's Rough Party!! `-1s` at 100x) is no longer drawn as a pop-up.
- **Kiai flash.** It follows stable: beats count from the first red line, the flash lasts 3/4 of a beat, and its reach is limited.
- **Autoplay and hitsounds.** Sliders written between -0.1 and 0.1 long are not played, so `+0.0001` stacks no longer make a loud noise and give no ticks. Drumroll ticks use osu!'s clamped beat length and slider velocity, which fixes a freeze when opening We Love Hardcore by katacheh at its preview point.
- **Ctrl+C timestamps.** Notes are numbered by combo, restarting on a new combo, after a spinner and after a break.
