# KatsuDon Editor v3.5.11

Drumroll ticks in the gameplay preview are coloured the way osu! stable colours them. A drumroll mapped across notes is played, and heard, as the notes alone.

## Updating to this version

From v3.4.2 or later, click **Update and Restart**. From v3.4.1 or earlier, download it by hand once; see the v3.4.2 notes.

## Fixed

- **Drumroll tick colours.** v3.5.10 tinted every tick yellow. Now a tick on the beat is yellow, one on the half beat pale orange, and the rest keep `sliderscorepoint`'s own colour, as in stable.
- **A drumroll mapped across notes.** When a real drumroll's span contains dons or kats, autoplay hits only the notes, and the roll's ticks don't sound over them. Its ticks used to be hit and heard on top of the notes.
