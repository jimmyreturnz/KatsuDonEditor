# KatsuDon Editor v3.6.3

Any playback rate with or without the pitch, combo numbers in the chart view, and songs that stay inside osu!'s audio rules.

## Updating to this version

From v3.4.2 or later, click **Update and Restart**. From v3.4.1 or earlier, download it by hand once; see the v3.4.2 notes.

## New

- **Custom playback rate.** The speed row (25% 50% 75% 100%) has a fifth button, **Custom**. It opens a slider from 25% to 200% and a **Change pitch** switch:
  - Off keeps the song's pitch, as DT and HT do. On moves the pitch with the speed, as NC and DC do.
  - The button shows the rate, with ♪ when the pitch moves. The mods chip in the header shows it beside the mods.
  - A rate a mod already names is that mod: 150% with the pitch kept lights DT, 75% with it moved lights DC. A rate a speed button names lights that button.
  - A mod pill or a speed button ends the custom rate. The rate is remembered between sessions.
  - The gameplay preview's kiai flash and hit animations follow the custom rate, as they follow DT and HT.
- **Combo numbers in the chart view.** These are osu!'s editor combo numbers, the ones a timestamp quotes. They count from 1 at every new combo, after a spinner and after a break.
  - They are drawn under the notes, not inside them, because a chart-view circle is too small for four digits.
  - When zoomed out, only 1 and every 2nd, 5th, 10th, 25th, 50th or 100th number is shown: the smallest step whose labels fit. The same notes stay labelled while the view scrolls.
  - A label that would touch the one before it is skipped, so a stack of fake sliders on one note shows one number.
  - The gimmick layers show none. Turn them off with **Settings → Appearance → Show combo numbers**.
- **Check whether a song is rankable** (Miscellaneous → Taiko Utilities). The check covers format (MP3 or Ogg Vorbis), average bitrate (at most 192 kbps for MP3, 208 for Ogg, at least 128) and sample rate (at most 48 kHz). It needs ffmpeg on PATH.
- **Make a rankable copy of the song.** This writes `<song> (rankable)` beside the original, inside those limits and never above the source's own bitrate. It never overwrites a file.

## Fixed

- **The lead-in tool and the Rate Changer write rankable audio.** They wrote LAME V0 (about 245 kbps) and Vorbis q8 (about 256 kbps), which is over the ranking criteria's cap on every song. Both now take the source's own bitrate, snapped to an MP3 CBR step and kept under the cap. The added silence is still sample-exact.
