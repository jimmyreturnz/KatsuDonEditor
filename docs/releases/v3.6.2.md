# KatsuDon Editor v3.6.2

Smoother scrolling on every monitor and a faster song list, plus bookmarks on the timing bar, a lead-in tool, and clap as the default kat.

## Updating to this version

From v3.4.2 or later, click **Update and Restart**. From v3.4.1 or earlier, download it by hand once; see the v3.4.2 notes.

## Fixed

- **Scrolling stutter, on fast PCs too.** Frames used to be timed by a 4 ms timer, so they could only land on a 4 ms grid, which never matches a monitor's refresh. A 100 Hz screen got frames 8 and 12 ms apart instead of 10, and a 144 Hz screen 8, 8, 8, 4. Frames now wait for the monitor's own vertical blank and are timed on its refresh grid, as taiko-rs does. Measured over 8 s at 99.93 Hz:
  - Before, the share of refreshes showing exactly one new frame varied from run to run, between 19% and 98%. The content step per refresh had a spread of 0.26 to 0.90.
  - After, 99% of refreshes show exactly one new frame, and the spread is 0.10 to 0.16.
- **Frames cost less with see-through views.** Below 100% View opacity, every frame repainted the whole page under the views: the map background, the page and every view frame, about ten layers of a full window at 1080p. The views now draw what shows through them themselves, with the same look, so only the views repaint. A frame at 1080p went from 4.1 to 2.5 ms at 60% opacity and from 3.8 to 2.2 ms at 100%. At 4K it went from 8.9 to 3.8 ms, which now fits a 120 Hz refresh, and a 240 Hz one most of the time.
- **The song list loads faster.** A set folder that hasn't changed since the last scan is no longer opened. Each of its files used to be listed and checked every launch. A warm scan of 24,698 difficulties took 2.66 s and now takes 0.49 s.
  - A file edited in place doesn't change its folder. Reload song or Rescan picks that edit up.
- **Heavy gimmick maps preview faster.** Unscaled sprites were drawn with smoothing on, which changes no pixel of them and made each draw 5x slower, and a barline wall is about 525 of them a frame. Walls of vertical lines (barlines, the SV graph, the snap grid) are now drawn as one row stretched down, and the preview only looks at the notes and lines near the screen. Gameplay preview paint at 1754x257 on three heavy gimmick maps went from 13.2 to 3.0 ms, 11.8 to 4.1 ms and 12.8 to about 6 ms. The look is unchanged.
- **Pop-up notes fade in again.** On maps with very slow gimmick lines, pop-up notes (fake sliders) took their timing from the slowest line, so they stood on screen for minutes and their fade-in was over before they were seen. They now use the red line right after their own.
- **Autoplay skips the notes osu! can't hit around NaN sliders.** A NaN-length slider throws off which notes stable can hit, and that is a run of circles, not only the note before it. The preview's autoplay now skips the same circles stable does, checked against stable on a 21-circle case.
- **Autoplay hits drumrolls as osu! does.** Drumroll ticks now follow osu!'s rule: 1/8 of the beat, doubled or halved until they are 60–120 ms apart. The old 1/4-beat rule agreed only between 63 and 500 BPM, and under a gimmick's 60000 BPM line it ticked every 1.5 ms. Rolls stacked on one place are hit and heard once per tick, not once per roll.

## New

- **Bookmarks on the timing bar.** Hover the bar and a bookmark row floats just above its left end, as in osu!'s editor. **+** adds a bookmark at the playhead, snapped. **−** removes the nearest one. **|◀** and **▶|** jump to the previous and next bookmark. **Reset** removes them all. The row covers what is under it and moves nothing in the layout. **Ctrl+B** and **Ctrl+Shift+B** do the same as + and −, and everything is undoable.
- **Add silence to the start of the song** (Miscellaneous → Taiko Utilities). For a first note that comes too soon, or plays late on a slow machine:
  - It writes a new song file with the silence before it, as MP3 or OGG through ffmpeg, or WAV without it.
  - Every difficulty that uses the song is switched to the new file and shifted later by the same amount.
  - The original file is kept, and undo restores both the chart and the song the editor plays.
  - Decoded back, the music in a 500 ms lead-in MP3 starts 0 samples off.
- **Song select remembers where you left the divider** between the song list and the background.
- **Clicking a note in the Editor page's gameplay preview selects it** in that difficulty's chart views.

## Changed

- **New kats are clap.** That applies to kats placed on the Editor page and to gimmick structures. "Every kat plays" on the Miscellaneous page starts on Clap. W and R still set whistle and clap.

## Known

- At 3840x2160 on a 240 Hz screen (4.17 ms a frame), the slowest 5% of frames (5 ms) still miss a refresh.
