# KatsuDon Editor v3.6.1

Fixes in the gameplay preview: fake sliders, drumroll and spinner fading, the kiai flash, and barlines past the last object. Gimmicks built from skin elements now show in the preview.

## Updating to this version

From v3.4.2 or later, click **Update and Restart**. From v3.4.1 or earlier, download it by hand once; see the v3.4.2 notes.

## Fixed

- **Long fake sliders show again.** A fake slider with a long negative length (a `-800`, for example) never appeared. It stopped seconds before its own time, off screen, and had faded out before reaching the screen. It now scrolls in as usual and stops no earlier than its own time. Its body and cap fade in as it approaches and arrive solid. A short fake slider's body still fades out after its cap reaches the hit position.
- **Drumroll and spinner fading.** A drumroll's body, cap and ticks fade out after its end, over two thirds of the map's approach time. Its head is gone at the end. A spinner fades in over the first two fifths of the approach time, so only a slow one is seen fading. The approach time comes from the map's first timing point.
- **The kiai flash is no longer too bright.** A new flash on an object replaces its last one instead of adding to it. Before, a plain note under a wall of red lines lit almost as brightly as a shiny note. A pile of fake sliders under one note still lights brighter the deeper it is, because each object keeps its own flash.
- **Kiai flash timing.** It fires on every beat of the red line in force, 5 ms early, and once more when a new red line takes over. It lasts 3/4 of that beat, shorter under DT and NC and longer under HT and DC. How far ahead of the beat it reaches is set in time, not pixels, so the preview's size does not change which notes flash.
- **Skin gimmicks show in the preview.** The kiai flash lights a drumroll's stretched `taiko-roll-middle` and its `taiko-roll-end`, not only the head.
- **No barlines past the last object.** Barlines stop 1 ms after the last object ends. A very slow slider at the end of a map no longer has barlines drawn behind it.
- **The drumroll colour** is `#fcb806`.
- **A NaN green line reads as 1.0x.**
