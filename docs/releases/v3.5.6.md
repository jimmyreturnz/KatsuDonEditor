# Taiko Fancy Arranger v3.5.6

A Miscellaneous page for set-wide chores, a Scale SV tool, red and green lines edited as a pair, and a fix for playback going silent after a seek on an .ogg.

## Updating to this version

From v3.4.2 or later, click **Update and Restart**. From v3.4.1 or earlier, download it by hand once; see the v3.4.2 notes.

## New

- **Miscellaneous page.** Three set-wide chores in one place: the beatmapset's metadata (one difficulty or all of them, blank fields left alone, tags counted against osu!'s 1000-character limit), shifting the whole map's offset (notes, timing lines, bookmarks and the preview point), and scaling every green line's SV. All of these are normal edits: Save writes them, and Ctrl+Z undoes them.
- **Scale SV** (`Ctrl+M`, and tool 4 on the SV graph). Multiplies the selected green lines' SV and then adds a number, so an effect you copied can be pasted somewhere else and rescaled. As a tool, it is a rubber band that scales whatever it catches.
- **Red and green lines on the same millisecond open as one dialog.** A red line now has an SV field. Before, double clicking that millisecond only ever reached the green line. Moving the pair keeps the green line after the red one, so the SV it sets survives.
- **Save picks the difficulties.** Ctrl+S saves the difficulty on screen, as in osu!'s editor. When more than one difficulty has changes, the header's Save asks which to write, and can discard one and reload it from disk.
- **Flat curve in Generate SV.** It holds one SV across the range. Across a BPM change, the Relative options decide whether the number or the scroll speed stays the same.
- **A pasted SV effect lands at the preset's Position offset**, like a generated one, whatever offset the copied lines had.
- **The image trace's sliders have boxes to type into**, and those boxes go past the slider's end (speck limit up to 500, smoothing up to 30px).
- **Start Menu search finds the app.** It writes its own shortcut on startup, and rewrites it if the folder moves.
- **Song select has a Reload button** beside the Songs folder (Reload this beatmap, Quick scan, Rescan everything). Click the folder itself to change it.

## Fixed

- **Playback went silent after a seek, then resumed from the wrong place.** On an .ogg the track decodes in the background, and a seek past the decoded part played nothing while the playhead ran on. The playhead now waits for the audio.
- **Generate SV across a red line lost the sweep on that note.** Its green line went `offset` early, and the red line reset it to 1.0x. On a red line's millisecond the green line now goes on the red, after it in file order.
- **Undoing a deleted red line could reset the SV** of a green line on the same millisecond, because the red line came back after it. It is restored to its old file position now.
- **One arrow or wheel notch moved an SV box by 1.0**, from 0.1x to 1.1x. Fractions now step by 0.01 and BPM by 1. Pattern parameters also step by 0.01.
- **DT/NC shortened the preview's hit animations** to two thirds of their length. They keep their nomod length now; the kiai pulse still follows the beat.
- **A converted gimmick on a note with its own BPM change left the note visible.** The chart's red line there is replaced, and its meter is carried over.
- **Generate SV's offset box jumped** each time Generate at was switched. Snap now sits below it.
- **Clicking the side list in a short settings sheet seemed to do nothing.** Sheets that fit on screen now show one section at a time.
