# Taiko Fancy Arranger v3.5.8

A Rate Changer on the Miscellaneous page, in the manner of osu-trainer: any difficulty at 0.8x to 1.5x (or any typed rate from 0.5 to 2), each as a new difficulty with its own song, and every SV gimmick still in place. Plus Ctrl+click selection.

## Updating to this version

From v3.4.2 or later, click **Update and Restart**. From v3.4.1 or earlier, download it by hand once; see the v3.4.2 notes.

## New

- **Rate Changer.** Writes each chosen rate as a new difficulty in the same set, named like `Oni 1.2x (264bpm)`.
  - **It follows osu!.** While the section is on screen it shows the difficulty selected in osu! (background, title, artist, difficulty, mapper, BPM, length, OD, HP), or the one being edited when osu! has nothing selected.
  - **Rates are buttons**, 0.8x to 1.5x, and any other rate from 0.5 to 2 can be typed and added. Each one is a row in a table with its BPM, length, OD, HP and name.
  - **Pitch: Keep or Change**, for every rate at once, as Double Time or Nightcore plays the song. Any row can change its own.
  - **OD: Manual or Scale.** Scale gives each rate the OD that makes its timing windows as strict as the rate mod would. OD and HP can be typed per row.
  - **Audio as MP3 or OGG**, written through ffmpeg if it is installed, else WAV. The song is stretched by the editor's own time-stretch rather than an external program.
  - **The notes stay on the music.** Measured on Libertas, the offset to the music after conversion is within 1 ms of the original's divided by the rate, at 0.75x, 1.2x and 1.5x, in MP3 and OGG.
  - **Gimmicks survive.** Every time is rounded to the nearest millisecond rather than truncated, and two objects 1 ms apart stay 1 ms apart, so the hide and restore lines of a barline note or a hidden anti-barline do not collapse onto one millisecond. osu-trainer merges 205 of 415 such pairs on `mekurume`; this merges none. Green lines are untouched, so SV is unchanged; breaks, bookmarks, the preview point and the video offset move with the rate.
  - **Rendered once.** A rate whose song is already in the folder (from another difficulty of the set) reuses it. Up to three rates render at once.
- **Ctrl+click adds or removes one note** from the selection, so the K's of `d kdd K d K` can be picked out; Ctrl+drag adds a box to it. Generate SV over a run then puts SV on the selected notes alone.
- **The window reopens at the size and place it was closed at.**

## Fixed

- **Shift offset now also moves breaks and the background video**, not only notes, lines, bookmarks and the preview point.
- **The Mappack Creator's background thumbnails covered their own frame**, which showed on two sides only. The image now sits inside it, and a thumbnail you cannot drop onto has a solid frame rather than a dashed one.
