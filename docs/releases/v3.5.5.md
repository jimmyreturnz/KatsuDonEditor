# Taiko Fancy Arranger v3.5.5

A small update: F5 reads a chart from its file again, Generate SV can hold the scroll speed of a BPM you type, and the Exp x exponent is remembered.

## Updating to this version

From v3.4.2 or later, click **Update and Restart**. From v3.4.1 or earlier, download it by hand once; see the v3.4.2 notes.

## New

- **Relative to BPM** in Generate SV, next to Relative to final BPM. Each rate is scaled by the BPM you type over the BPM where the point lands, so 1.0x scrolls as 1.0x does at that BPM everywhere in the range, including inside a 60000 BPM gimmick run. The box starts at the BPM where the selection begins. Ticking one of the two options unticks the other. Your choice (final, typed or off) and the BPM are remembered; on a gimmick layer both start off.
- **Remember last choice for the Exp x exponent.** A tuned exponent comes back even after sweeps on other curves.
- **F5 in the editor** re-reads the current chart from its .osu, after asking first. It can be rebound in Settings.

## Fixed

- **A chart saved in osu!'s editor reopened as this app's copy.** A difficulty opened earlier in the session stayed in memory, so reloading showed that version instead of the file. F5 on song select now also re-reads the song's open difficulties. If one has unsaved edits, it asks before discarding them.
