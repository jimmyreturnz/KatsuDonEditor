# Taiko Fancy Arranger v3.5.9

The Rate Changer works without ffmpeg again. It also takes a BPM instead of a rate, and every number in its table can be typed. On the Gimmick page, one copy now takes the selection from every layer.

## Updating to this version

From v3.4.2 or later, click **Update and Restart**. From v3.4.1 or earlier, download it by hand once; see the v3.4.2 notes.

## Fixed

- **"Not converted: mode must be r, rb, w, or wb".** Without ffmpeg installed, the Rate Changer writes WAV, and that path failed every time. It now writes the WAV and still refuses to overwrite a file that is already there.

## New

- **Rate Changer**
  - **A BPM instead of a rate.** Type 320 on a 269 BPM map and the rate is worked out for you.
  - **Any rate up to 1000 BPM** on the chart's main BPM. The old limit was 0.5x to 2x.
  - **Everything in the table can be typed:** rate, BPM, OD, HP and the difficulty name. Change the rate or the BPM and the other follows, along with the length and, under Scale, the OD.
  - **Columns can be resized**, and each row has a ✕ to remove it. The rate buttons follow the table.
  - **Names from a template.** Use `{name}`, `{rate}` and `{bpm}` in any order with any words, for example `Distracting at 300BPM`, `Distracting at 300BPM (1.2x)` or `Distracting at 1.2x`. The Name, Rate and BPM buttons insert them. The default is the old `{name} {rate}x ({bpm}bpm)`. A name typed into a row overrides the template.
  - **Two difficulties with one name are refused before anything renders**, as is a name equal to the original's.
  - **The header is song select's banner**: the background, title and artist in the same font.
- **One copy across gimmick layers.** On the Gimmick page, Ctrl+C takes the selection from every layer of the difficulty. Notes picked in the note layer and a barline structure in the barline layer paste together, as one undo step.
- **The tab switch sound can be turned off** in Settings > Audio > Interface.

## Known

- **Past 1.5x the notes land about 8 ms late against the music**, and at 0.5x about 8 ms early. On Libertas the expected offsets are 7 ms at 2.5x and 5 ms at 3.5x, and 15 and 13 ms were measured. From 0.75x to 1.5x the result is still within 1 ms of the original's offset divided by the rate. The stretch's timing correction has only been measured up to 1.5x.
