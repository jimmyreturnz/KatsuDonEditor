# KatsuDon Editor v3.5.10

Taiko Fancy Arranger is now **KatsuDon Editor** (カツ丼). The gameplay preview draws more gimmicks the way osu! stable does: pop-up fake sliders, NaN sliders, spinner fade-in, a map's own spinner art, and charts with notes that barely move. W and E now switch Don/Kat and Finisher, and the window snaps to half a 1080p screen.

## Updating to this version

From v3.4.2 or later, click **Update and Restart**. Your settings and song index carry over. The old Start Menu entry is replaced by one named KatsuDon Editor. From v3.4.1 or earlier, download it by hand once; see the v3.4.2 notes.

## New

- **The new name**, in the window title, dialogs, Start Menu, the exe (`KatsuDonEditor.exe`) and the release zip.
- **W and E**
  - **W** on a selection makes every circle a kat (whistle), or a don again if all are kats already. **R** does the same with clap. With nothing selected, W switches between the Don and Kat tools.
  - **E** makes the selection a finisher, or toggles the new **Finisher** tool. It is Shift held down for good. Finisher is tool 4 on the Editor page and sits after Kat in the Gimmick page's chart and fake slider layers.
  - W, E and R also work in the Gimmick page's chart and fake slider layers.
  - A placed kat is whistle, not clap.
- **Taiko Utilities page:** Offset, SV and a new **Kat hitsound** tool (every kat whistle or clap, finishers kept) on one page.
- **Ctrl+C also copies osu!'s timestamp**, like `00:01:000 (1,2) - `, so it can be pasted into a beatmap discussion.
- **A smaller window.** Only the page on show sets how small the window can go. Rows that don't fit scroll sideways instead of holding the window open, so it snaps to half a 1080p screen.
- **The speed buttons are in the status bar**, left of View size, on the Editor and Gimmick pages, so the timing bar is wider.
- **Applied Artist and Title** show in the header and song select.
- **The updater shows the release note as it is now**, rendered with its formatting, rather than the copy taken when the release was published.

## Gameplay preview

- **Pop-up fake sliders** (senpai, notice me! [w]). A long fake slider on a 1 ms red line stands still mid-lane, farther out the longer it is. It shows |length| / (100 × SliderMultiplier × SV) beats before its time, fades in, and is gone at its time, as in stable.
- **NaN sliders** (`…,1,NaN`) are invisible in the preview, as in osu!. The chart still shows them, with "NaN" written inside.
- **Spinners fade in** from 1.5 beats before (ppy/osu#21769). When the map folder has its own `spinner-warning.png`, the spinner draws as that at small-note size, over the notes.
- **Drumroll ticks** (`sliderscorepoint`) every 1/4 beat (1/8 at 125 BPM or slower, 1/2 above 250 BPM). Ticks on the 1/4 grid are tinted the roll's yellow; the 1/8 ticks in between keep the image's own colour.
- **On one millisecond, a fake slider draws over the don or kat.**
- **A short fake slider's cap passes through the hit position** like a real drumroll's. Long ones still lose head and cap when the cap lands.
- **Notes that barely move are on screen as early as they should be.** A fake slider at SV 0.02 can be on screen 40 seconds before it lands, and nothing caps how far ahead the preview looks.
- **A map's own skin images** apply in the chart views too.
- The drumroll's cap is as tall as its track.

## Fixed

- **A drag lands the grabbed note on the current snap.** A note on a grid the setting doesn't contain used to move along its own grid, so switching to 1/3 could never put a 1/4 note on a triplet.
